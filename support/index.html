Lugsy Support

We are happy to help.

Get in touch. Email abi@grillz.studio. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when something went wrong. We reply as soon as we can.

Lugsy did not find a trip in my photos

Lugsy finds trips from when and where photos were taken, so photos without a location cannot be placed. A trip also needs a few photos away from home. Tap "Check for new photos" on My photo trips, or build the trip yourself with "Pick the cities myself".

A place has the wrong name, or no name

Tap the place on the trip screen or in the route and type the name you want. Lugsy uses it for that spot from then on.

I do not want my photos in the video

Open the trip, tap the photos row, and switch off "Photos in this video". You can also tap any photo to swap or remove it.

I gave access to only some photos

Lugsy can only find trips in the photos it can see. To change this, open Settings on your phone, then Apps, Lugsy, Photos, and choose Full Access.

The video did not save

Saving needs permission to add to your photo library. Open Settings on your phone, then Apps, Lugsy, Photos, and allow adding photos.

Privacy

Your photos and trips stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
