Lugsy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version. Your photos and your trips stay on your phone. Lugsy has no accounts and shows no ads. We collect anonymous usage statistics so we can see which parts of the app work and which do not. If you allow it when your phone asks, we also let Meta know that an ad of ours led to an install or a purchase, so we can tell which of our ads work.

Who we are

Lugsy is made by Grillz App Studio Inc. ("we", "us"). You can reach us at abi@grillz.studio.

What stays on your phone

  • Your photos. If you allow access to your photo library, Lugsy reads when and where your photos were taken to find your trips, and looks at the photos of a trip you open to choose which ones to show. All of this happens on your phone. Your photos, their dates and their locations are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your trips and videos. The trips you build, the names you give places, and the videos Lugsy makes are stored on your phone. A video leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself.
  • Your location. If you tap "Start where I am", Lugsy asks your phone for your approximate location once, to suggest the city a trip starts in. It is used on your phone and is not sent to us.

What we collect

Lugsy sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog. These tell us what was done in the app and how much of it, for example that a video was made, how many stops it had, how long it was, and whether it was exported.

These events include:

  • a random identifier created by the app, which is not your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier;
  • your device model, iOS version, language, time zone and the app version;
  • your IP address, which PostHog uses to work out an approximate country and city and to deliver the data.

These events never include city or trip names, dates, coordinates, or anything about a photo.

Purchases

Lugsy Pro is a subscription bought through Apple. We never see your card or your Apple ID. RevenueCat, Inc. keeps a record of the purchase against a random identifier created by the app, so that Lugsy knows you are subscribed and can restore it on a new phone.

Measuring our ads

We advertise Lugsy on Facebook and Instagram. To know which ads bring people to the app, Lugsy includes Meta's software, which tells Meta that the app was installed and opened, and that a few things happened in it: a video was made, a video was exported, or the subscription screen was shown. RevenueCat tells Meta when a free trial or a subscription starts. Meta receives your device model, iOS version, IP address and a random identifier with these.

Your phone asks whether Lugsy may track you. If you say yes, Meta also receives your phone's advertising identifier, and can match the install to an ad you saw in another app; that is tracking, and it is used to measure and aim our advertising. If you say no, the advertising identifier is not sent and the reports to Meta are not linked to you. You can change your answer at any time in Settings, under Privacy and Security, then Tracking. Nothing about your photos, trips, or places is ever part of this.

How we use it

We use the usage statistics to understand how Lugsy is used, to fix problems and to decide what to improve. We use what is shared with Meta only to measure and improve our own advertising. We do not sell your information.

Who processes it

PostHog, Inc. stores and processes the usage events on our behalf, on servers in the United States. RevenueCat, Inc. processes subscription records on our behalf. Meta Platforms, Inc. receives the advertising measurement described above and handles it under its own data policy. Apple may provide us with aggregate download and crash statistics under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Usage events are kept for as long as they are useful for the purposes above, and are deleted or anonymised after that. Everything stored on your phone is removed when you delete the app.

Your choices

  • You can use Lugsy without giving access to your photos or your location. You can change either at any time in Settings on your phone.
  • You can ask us to delete the usage data linked to your app's identifier, or ask what we hold, by writing to the address above. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA; we will honour them.

Children

Lugsy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top. If Lugsy adds accounts, this page will say what that involves before it does.
